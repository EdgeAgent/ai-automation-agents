# services — Edge Agency services

Edge Agency builds AI automation for small businesses. Services include AI
receptionists, lead list building, and workflow automation with n8n and
Make. The AI receptionist answers calls, books appointments, and sends a
summary after each call.

# pricing — Pricing and first pack

The first lead pack costs $50 and includes 5 leads built to the buyer's
criteria. Regular packs cost $149. A monthly pipeline costs $450 for 25
leads.

# delivery — Delivery and turnaround

Lead packs are delivered by email within two business days. Every lead
includes the owner's contact phone number.
